Privacy Policy

TV Tracker · Last updated 27 October 2026

TV Tracker is a personal, offline-first app for tracking the shows, movies, and sports you watch. It is built to keep your data on your own device. This policy explains what is stored, where, and when anything leaves your browser.

What we store, and where

  • Your library and activity — the titles you track, watch history, ratings, favorites, badges, and settings — are stored locally in your browser (IndexedDB). They never touch our servers. Clearing your browser data or site storage deletes them.
  • We have no accounts and no server database. There is nothing to sign up for, and we do not collect, sell, or share personal information.
  • No analytics, advertising, or third-party trackers are used.

Optional Google Drive sync

If you choose to connect Google Drive, the app saves a single backup file to your own Google Drive using the drive.appdata scope. That scope restricts the app to a private, app-specific folder — it cannot see or access any of your other Drive files. Your backup stays in your Google account; we never receive or store it. Your Google display name and profile picture are shown in the app only and are not transmitted elsewhere. You can disconnect at any time, and you can revoke access from your Google account permissions.

Optional notifications

If you enable push notifications for upcoming episodes, your browser's push subscription (an anonymous endpoint, not your identity) and the next reminder times are stored by a self-hosted Cloudflare Worker so it can send “airing soon” reminders. No watch history or personal data is sent. Disabling notifications removes the subscription.

Third-party services

To show metadata, air times, reviews, and scores, your browser makes requests directly to the services below. Each receives your IP address and the specific request (for example, a title id) as part of normal operation, under its own privacy policy. TV Tracker sends them no account or identifying information.

  • TMDB — movie & TV metadata (served through our proxy, which adds no personal data).
  • TVmaze — episode air times.
  • Trakt — community reviews and optional sync (only if you connect it).
  • ESPN — sports schedules and scores.
  • Wikidata — related-title / franchise links.
  • IMDb — outbound links only; no data is sent unless you click through.

For more on the data and APIs these services provide, see Attributions.

Children's privacy

TV Tracker is a general-audience tool and is not directed to children under 13, and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

This policy may be updated as the app changes; the “last updated” date above will reflect any revision.
