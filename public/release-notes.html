Release Notes

TV Tracker · Last updated 12 November 2026

Release 1.0.6

  • You can now build your own Lists. Group shows, movies, and sports teams however you like — favorites, a watch‑party lineup, a themed collection — and find them all on your Stats page. Each list gets its own page with a poster and backdrop you can upload, plus a name and description. To add titles, open a list and search: tap any result to drop it in. Lists back up and sync across your devices with everything else. Favorite a list to add it to your Library.
  • Personalize your Stats page with your own avatar and backdrop. A custom avatar also takes the place of the default photo everywhere else in the app.
  • Importing a TV Time export now brings your custom lists across too, matched up to the same shows and movies already in your library.
  • You can now add the random result from your Search page to your Favorites, or to a list.
  • Opening a show or team now jumps its episode or game list straight to your next unwatched one, so you don’t have to scroll through a long season to find where you left off.
  • Shows you’re watching now show their next episode’s air date right away, instead of briefly reading “Nothing scheduled” until the full episode details finish loading.
  • Refreshing the app no longer signs you out of Google Drive — a still-valid sign-in is reused across a refresh instead of prompting you to sign in again.
  • Added anonymous, privacy-friendly usage counts so we can see roughly how many people use TV Tracker — since there are no accounts, there was no way to know before. It’s a once-a-day ping with a random id: no name, email, or anything about what you watch. Details in the Privacy Policy.

Release 1.0.5

  • First-time setup is faster and stores far less data on your device — the on-device episode cache is now a fraction of its previous size.
  • A large library’s first sync is more reliable: the errors that could pile up near the end are fixed, and episode air times load without overloading your connection.
  • The Upcoming tab refreshes using less network traffic.
  • Added a Stop button to the episode index in Settings, so you can pause a long indexing pass — it resumes where it left off.
  • Signing in to Google Drive or resetting your account no longer floods the screen with already-earned badges; only newly earned ones celebrate.
  • Added a Share button on show, movie, episode, and team pages. It copies a link you can send to someone — opening it takes them to that title, where they can add it to their own library.
  • You can now change the watched date of any item — including sporting events — right from the Timeline: tap the calendar icon on a row and pick a new date.

Release 1.0.4

  • Signing in to Google Drive is now instant — your library syncs in the background instead of making you wait on the welcome screen.
  • Sporting events now show their start time on the team page, in the Upcoming tab, and in airing-soon notifications.
  • Fixed sporting events sometimes showing on the wrong day (an evening game could appear as the next day).
  • Fixed an episode airing later today briefly showing under “Recently aired” in the evening.
  • The Upcoming tab now lists items from the same day in start-time order.
  • Tracked teams’ schedules now refresh automatically in the background, so game times and dates stay current without opening each team’s page.

Release 1.0.3

  • Added a confirmation prompt when marking a season's episodes (or games) as watched, and the ability to clear all watched episodes when changing a show's status away from Complete.
  • Added filtering for the Talk, News, Soap, and Music genres. TV shows can now also be matched by the Science Fiction filter — note that TV lumps sci-fi into a single "Sci-Fi & Fantasy" genre, so you may not be able to filter Science Fiction and Fantasy separately.

Release 1.0.2

  • Always show ad-supported services (Tubi, The Roku Channel, etc.) in addition to paid ones.
  • Added broadcaster information for sporting events (e.g. “on CBS”, “on Prime Video”).
  • NASCAR series events can now be tracked.
  • The Search window now includes a Random button to help you pick an item from your Watching list.

Release 1.0.1

  • Added a Hiatus group to separate items that are not currently airing from your Watching list.
  • Now using Trakt to pull airing times for episodes.

Release 1.0.0

  • Initial public release! Welcome to TV Tracker.
